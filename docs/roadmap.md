# Research Roadmap

## v0.1 (this release) — Deferral Engine
Structural model, four scenarios, invariant-checked accounting, KPI suite,
Monte Carlo + sensitivity infrastructure, dashboard, governance, CI.

## v0.2 — Empirical Calibration + Efficient Frontier
- Calibrate household wealth distribution (SCF/DFA), realization behavior
  (IRS SOI/CBO), MPC by cohort, asset returns and wage growth.
- Investigate securities-backed lending data.
- Large Monte Carlo experiments with uncertainty bands.
- Global sensitivity analysis (e.g., Sobol indices) to identify which
  assumptions dominate outcomes.
- Estimate the **Circular Capitalism efficient frontier**: the policy region
  jointly evaluated on output/growth, median wealth, capital formation,
  recirculation, wealth concentration, fiscal revenue, and (once modeled)
  mobility. The objective is not "maximize taxes" — it is to find
  configurations that sustain circulation and broad prosperity while
  preserving incentives for productive capital formation.

## v0.3+ candidates
- Estate channel: step-up at death ("buy, borrow, die" complete).
- Progressive tax schedules; multiple asset classes; inflation.
- Wealth mobility metrics; demographic turnover.
- Firm entry/exit and credit-market feedback.
- Broad-based ownership mechanisms.

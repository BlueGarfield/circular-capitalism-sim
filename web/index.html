Circular Capitalism Simulator

Browser-only companions to the open-source research repository.

Society ViewOverhead isometric city driven by the simulation — wealth as height, deferred gains as glow, debt as blue, dividends as pulses. Chart LabScenario comparison with policy sliders: top 1% share, median wealth, Gini, deferred wealth stock. Illustrative synthetic simulations under stylized, uncalibrated assumptions — not empirical estimates of any real economy. Evidence-grade runs: ccsim-batch in the repository.
